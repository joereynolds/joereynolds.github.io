First Grisham book and felt like I made a good choice.

Super easy reading and extremely gripping. I have a feeling his other books are
quite similar so I'm not sure how many I could read before it gets samey.

I will be reading A Time to Kill since people seem to rave about that.

If you want some revenge porn that shits on the big guy then this'll easily
tick that box

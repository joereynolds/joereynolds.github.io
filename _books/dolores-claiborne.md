The weakest Stephen King book I've read so far. It leaned way too far into the
hick slant and just came off a bit "meh". I will say that the book is massively
better than the film and the book didn't exactly shine.

I doubt I'd ever read this again but I kind of had to just because it's Stephen
King. Oh and because my girlfriend got it me as a gift, heh.

I have just finished reading this so it's pretty fresh in my mind.

The writing is a bit difficult sometimes. Forgotten commas all over the shop
and then I find myself halfway through a sentence and still not comprehending
it.

Also it pains me to say this but I found the movie more enjoyable. I know this
is a man's personal account of all the death that went on in his city, but yeah, the movie
displayed it in a way that appeals to me more... sorry.

Had I not seen the movie first multiple times then maybe I would've rated this higher.

It's a depressing read so if that's your bag (it's always mine), I still think
you'll have a good time.

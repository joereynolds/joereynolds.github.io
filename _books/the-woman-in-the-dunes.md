One of the most original books I've ever read.

I would say you should read this just because of how unique it is.

The story is based around a woman that falls into a sandy hole and is forced into working there.

Just really strange and kind of refreshing. It's not another "slay this dragon or die"
or "my husband left me so I cooked him" story.

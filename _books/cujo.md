Perfection.

I almost gave this a 4.5 because it seemed like it couldn't get any better
towards the end but it did.

I read that Stephen King can't remember writing this book because he was on a
coke-rampage. That seems pretty plausible given the content of it. Then again,
it reads like most King books, which is a good thing.

The parallel stories of all the characters and how they all join back into
eachother is very well done and this is probably one of the only endings to a
book that felt satisfying to me.

In terms of King novels, this is definitely up there for me. Maybe one step
behind IT.

Also I hate Steve Kemp.

Pat Martino was in a heavy state of decline as I was writing this piece so I
dedicated this to him. It's nothing like a Pat Martino piece but I did steal
some of his shell voicings which transferred across for the main melody.

If you don't know who Pat Martino is, he's one of the best Jazz guitarists you
could listen to. Go and listen to Impressions. I use a particular idea of his
in almost all my improvsations.

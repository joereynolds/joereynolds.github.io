Titled after a trailing green XLR cable I had in my flat, I'm
very imaginative.

Julian Lage gave me pointers on this song too (he was giving
lessons through Covid, highly recommend if we ever face
another global disaster), particularly around the harmonic
rhythm of the song. The song's chord changes are now a little
less predicatable than they once were.

I'm not a huge fan of this song, it's not explored enough but
there's some decent ideas in here. Definitely some Avishai
Cohen influences cropping up on the melody halfway through
too.

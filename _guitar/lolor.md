One of my more popular songs. It features in a game called One Thousand Lies
which drove quite a lot of traffic to the video.

This songs is actually pretty basic. It centers around a consistent picking
pattern that doesn't change throughout and then cycles through some I V's and
minor I V's. The only gotcha is some weird minor third modulation towards the
end before heading back to the main melody. I got that from Paul Anthony Romero
after playing thousands of hours of Heroes of Might and Magic II back in the
day.

I sometimes read the comments to make me feel good, they're very wholesome and
encouraging. 

Also, my picking hand absolutely ripped in my early 20s, thankfully I've kept
most of that, heh.
